## Customer DPA: clauses for counsel to settle

**LAW starting point:** [K01 s42](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf) addresses instructed processing/written contract. [K02 rr24–25](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf) cover processing particulars, instructions, confidentiality/security, termination return/deletion, audit and authorized onward processing. The following is a **proposed drafting checklist**, not a representation of existing contracted promises:

1. Correct parties/proprietor; product/territories and purpose-specific role schedule; subject matter, duration, categories of people/data, permitted purposes and controller rights/duties. Identify excluded high-risk processing and how unintended free-text data is handled.
2. Documented lawful instructions, authorized customer contacts, instruction conflicts/escalation and disclosure demands; no unauthorized reuse/marketing/training or platform business-access entitlement.
3. Confidential personnel/support authorization and security annex tied to actual measures, not future RLS/purge/live export claims; verify identity, MFA, tenant scopes and infrastructure access separately.
4. Actual subprocessor list and prior-authorization/change/objection arrangements, flow-down responsibilities, overseas access/transfer terms and incident contacts. Assess whether each vendor is processor or independent controller for each purpose.
5. Rights assistance, controller coordination, proportionate verification, response ownership and clocks; no contractual routing that silently discards Stravect's own duties. Distinguish customer data export, individual access and portability.
6. Incident escalation, assessment/evidence, controller/processor notices and cooperation terms. Review customer contractual deadlines against applicable law before promising them; no live delivery capability inferred from notification simulations.
7. Return/deletion choice on termination, ordinary 7/60/90 policy versus legal/financial exceptions and holds, active artifact cleanup, metadata/dedup retention and backup restore quarantine. Resolve the contractual termination clause versus missing deletion implementation before signature; do not promise unconditional destruction.
8. Audit/inspection and evidence, assistance with impact assessments, data accuracy/minimization, retention review, change control, final approval and contract custody. Liability/indemnity, remedies, governing law/disputes and future incorporation/assignment need counsel rather than technical defaults.

No optional marketing consent or policy acknowledgment substitutes for purpose-specific lawful processing. Store decisions as activity/basis/authority/evidence/reviewer/date/conditions with linked supplier and retention rows. No basis is approved merely because a candidate appears here. **Stop for legal review.**
