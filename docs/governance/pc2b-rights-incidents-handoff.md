## Incident assessment and notifications

**LAW:** [DPA K01 s43](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf) distinguishes controller and processor duties. For unauthorized access/acquisition with real risk of harm: controller → ODPC without delay, within 72 hours of awareness; written subject communication within a reasonably practical period subject to statutory qualifications. Processor → controller without delay and, where reasonably practicable, within 48 hours. Delayed ODPC notice needs reasons; phased information and recordkeeping are addressed separately. These are statutory research findings, not approved notification execution.

K02 rr37–38 and Second Schedule add notifiable-category/report-content tests. Counsel must apply them to actual categories rather than assume a generic risk score alone controls. Review s43 qualifications (including appropriate security safeguards/encryption and offence-investigation communication restrictions); encryption is not a blanket exemption from ODPC reporting, and an app artifact encryption feature does not prove all affected data was protected.

| Scenario / decision | Role and proposed handler | Minimum review/evidence before approved execution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suspected breach vs ordinary service error | Founder + technical lead, with backup coverage | Preserve discovery/awareness timestamps and minimized facts, systems, data categories/people, access scope, containment and uncertainty. P-C1 defect is not proof of a past breach. Assess destruction/loss/alteration as well as disclosure. |
| Customer operational data affected | Stravect may be processor; customer controller must be coordinated with | Identify actual controller(s), prompt processor notice, contractual terms, incident facts and assistance. Do not wait 48 hours as a target; separate own-controller processing if also affected. |
| Account/billing/security/support data affected | Stravect may be controller | Assess statutory risk/category tests, affected subjects and relevant regulator(s); record decisions, sources, deadlines and phased facts. Customer notice is not a substitute for own-controller duties. |
| Supplier incident / overseas processing | Supplier owner + founder/legal | Actual supplier role, contractual reports, affected data/countries and onward parties; assess each clock independently. Supplier acceptance/receipt is not evidence customers were notified. |
| Notification required or not required | Counsel + accountable controller decision | Protected rationale, thresholds/exceptions, recipients, approved content/channel and actual evidence. No live sending or customer communication authorized by P-C2B. |
| Recovery / restoration | Technical lead + incident owner | Preserve evidence, verify restored access and independent deletion/UNKNOWN/dedup/suppression reconciliation; no automatic sending from an old backup. Review lessons and reopen risk assessment. |

Founder-led response is a business position, not a functioning on-call system. Appoint backup, technical lead, provider/customer contacts and evidence custodian; agree immediate escalation and pre-deadline legal availability. Rehearse with synthetic data before claiming capability. No actual incident register, case or communications were created here.

Procedure response/investigation periods applying to a regulator must not be misrepresented as Stravect's rights-response deadline.
