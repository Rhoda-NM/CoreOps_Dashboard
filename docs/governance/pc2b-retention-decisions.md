## Existing positions and legal anchors

**CHOICE (already approved):** ordinary operational data uses paid coverage expiry E, grace through E+7 exclusive, cancellation E+60 and 90 days thereafter (E+150) as controlled-deletion **eligibility**, subject to reviewed exceptions. **FACT-S/I:** [UTC calculation](/home/rmuya/rhoda-work/coreops_dashboard/src/server/lifecycle/policy.ts:10) and rehearsal-only [enforcement](/home/rmuya/rhoda-work/coreops_dashboard/src/server/lifecycle/config.ts:5) exist; deployed enforcement and controlled deletion do not follow from them. Never-paid/suspended/closed cases lack approved substitute triggers. A privacy erasure request can arise before E+150; counsel must reconcile individual rights and lawful exceptions without treating the commercial schedule as an automatic refusal.

Legal pinpoints for review: [K01 ss39–40](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf), [K02 rr19,35](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf). **Research lead, not approved schedule:** [KRA guidance K07](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/2174-kra%E2%80%99s-perspective-on-the-burden-and-standard-of-proof-in-tax-and-customs-cases) describes at least five-year tax-record retention. Accountant/counsel must verify current Tax Procedures Act s23, exact reporting-period trigger, tax-specific rules and amended assessment/proceeding extensions; current full statutory text was not retrieved (K08). No five-year blanket applies to all customer data, logs or exports, and no company-specific accounting rule is assumed to apply to a sole proprietor.

## Exception and approval contract for reviewers

**PROPOSAL:** each hold/exception decision needs opaque ID, lawful authority and purpose, narrowly scoped categories/fields/stores, trigger, required evidence, approver, permitted access/use, start/review/expiry or release condition, affected deletion jobs and provider/backup treatment. Record reasons for continuation/release and avoid unbounded default extensions. A hold retains necessary evidence; it does not automatically restore workspace access, grant export authority or permit marketing. A customer instruction conflicting with a Stravect obligation requires counsel-led resolution, not silent data destruction or a blanket refusal.

For each T-row record: founder choice, counsel determination, accountant confirmation where relevant, customer instruction, source/pinpoint/version, approved duration/trigger, minimal fields, exception precedence, periodic review and disposal evidence. Empty/unresolved rows remain blockers, not indefinite retention entitlements. Test later with synthetic populated fixtures including shared users, immutable financial history, expired artifacts, active leases and backup restores. Retention eligibility, due cleanup, proven deletion/anonymization and inability to resurrect data are distinct outcomes. **Stop for founder, legal and accounting review.**
